# General questions

## Do I need a referral?
No. You can book directly with any NetraCare doctor.

## I'm not sure which doctor to see.
Tell the assistant or the front desk what your concern is. If it isn't clear, we'll book a general eye
check-up with Dr. Kavya Nair, who will refer you within the clinic if needed.

## Can I book for my child or a parent?
Yes. Parents/legal guardians book for patients under 18. You can manage appointments for an adult family
member if they have authorised you at the front desk.

## What should I bring?
Photo ID, insurance card (if any), current glasses, all medicines and eye drops you use, and previous eye reports.

## Can the assistant tell me what is wrong with my eye?
No. The assistant can answer questions about the clinic, help you book, and point you to the right
specialist. Only a doctor can examine you and give medical advice.

## Lab and test reports
The assistant can tell you when a report is ready. Your doctor will review and explain it with you.
