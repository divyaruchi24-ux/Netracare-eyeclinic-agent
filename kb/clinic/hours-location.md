# Clinic hours, location and contact

**Address (fictional):** NetraCare Eye Clinic, 12 Lakeview Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038
**OPD hours:** Monday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00 (lunch break 13:00–14:00). Closed on Sundays and clinic holidays.
**Front desk phone (fictional):** +91 80 0000 1234
**Emergency line (fictional, 24×7):** +91 80 0000 9999
**National emergency number:** 112

## Parking and access
Basement parking is available. The clinic is wheelchair accessible with a lift to all floors. Large-print
forms are available at the front desk.

## Appointment slots
Routine and follow-up visits are booked in 15-minute slots; new-patient and surgery consultations take 30 minutes.
