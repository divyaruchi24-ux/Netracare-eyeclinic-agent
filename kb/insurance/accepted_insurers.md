# Accepted insurance and payment options

NetraCare works with the following **fictional** insurers and TPAs for cashless or reimbursement claims:

| Insurer / TPA | Cashless | Reimbursement |
|---|---|---|
| Suraksha Health Insurance | Yes | Yes |
| Arogya Plus General | Yes | Yes |
| NetraShield Mediclaim | No | Yes |
| Kaveri TPA Services | Yes | Yes |
| Corporate employee plans via Kaveri TPA | Yes | Yes |

## What is usually covered
Cataract surgery and medically necessary procedures are commonly covered, subject to your policy terms.

## What is usually not covered
Refractive surgery for spectacle independence (LASIK, SMILE, PRK), routine spectacles and cosmetic procedures.

## How to use insurance
Bring your insurance card, a photo ID and your policy number. For cashless claims, pre-authorisation takes
1–3 working days; the front desk will help you start it. Coverage decisions are made by the insurer, not the clinic.

## Payment options
Cash, UPI, debit/credit cards. EMI options are available for surgery packages.
