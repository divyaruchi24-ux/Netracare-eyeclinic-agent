# Dilated eye exam — what to expect

## What is it?
Drops are used to widen (dilate) the pupils so the doctor can examine the inside of the eye.

## How long does it take?
The drops take 20–40 minutes to work. Plan for **2–3 hours** at the clinic in total.

## After the exam
- Vision may be blurry and sensitive to light for 4–6 hours.
- **Do not drive yourself home.** Bring a driver or use a taxi.
- Sunglasses help with glare.

## Which visits usually include dilation?
Retina visits, diabetic eye screening, cataract consultations, many children's visits and first
comprehensive check-ups.
