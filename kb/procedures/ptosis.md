# Droopy eyelid (ptosis) and oculoplasty — FAQ

## Who do I see?
Eyelid, tear-duct and other oculoplasty concerns are seen by **Dr. Kavya Nair**.
Children with a droopy eyelid are usually seen first by **Dr. Arjun Menon** (Pediatric).

## What happens at the consultation?
An examination of eyelid position and function, photographs for your record, and a discussion of options.
Whether any procedure is appropriate is decided only by the doctor.

## Sudden drooping
A droopy eyelid that appears suddenly, especially with double vision, headache or weakness elsewhere, needs
emergency medical attention — do not wait for a clinic appointment.
