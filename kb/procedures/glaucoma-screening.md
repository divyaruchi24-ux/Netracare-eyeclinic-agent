# Glaucoma screening — FAQ

## What is glaucoma screening?
A set of painless tests that look at eye pressure, the optic nerve and the field of vision. Screening does
not by itself mean someone has glaucoma; results are interpreted only by the doctor.

## Who should consider screening?
People commonly book a screening if they have a family history of glaucoma, have been told their eye
pressure was "borderline" at another clinic, or simply want a check-up. Your doctor will advise how often to
return.

## Who do I see?
**Dr. Meera Iyer** (Glaucoma).

## What tests are done?
- Eye pressure measurement (tonometry)
- Optic nerve imaging (OCT)
- Visual field test (adds about 30 minutes)

## How should I prepare?
Bring any previous reports and all current eye-drop bottles. Continue your usual medications unless your
doctor has told you otherwise.
