# LASIK and refractive surgery — FAQ

## What is LASIK?
LASIK is a laser procedure that reshapes the clear front surface of the eye (the cornea) to reduce
dependence on glasses or contact lenses. NetraCare also offers SMILE and PRK. Which option, if any, is
suitable can only be decided by the surgeon after a full evaluation.

## Who do I see?
Refractive evaluations are done by **Dr. Ananya Rao** (Cornea & Refractive Surgery).

## What happens at the evaluation?
- A 30-minute new-patient visit with detailed corneal scans and measurements.
- Dilating drops may be used, so plan not to drive afterwards.
- Soft contact lenses must be paused beforehand (see *Contact lens pause before evaluation*).

## How long does the procedure take?
The laser part usually takes a few minutes per eye; plan for about 2 hours at the clinic on the day.

## What does it cost?
See the current *NetraCare price list*. The evaluation fee is charged separately from the procedure.

## Is it covered by insurance?
Refractive surgery for spectacle independence is usually **not** covered by insurers. See *Accepted insurance*.
