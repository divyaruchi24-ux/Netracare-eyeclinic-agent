# Diabetic eye screening — FAQ

## What is it?
A dilated examination and retinal imaging for people living with diabetes. How often to repeat it is decided
by the doctor.

## Who do I see?
**Dr. Vikram Shetty** (Retina & Vitreous).

## How should I prepare?
- Eat and take your usual diabetes medicines as normal unless your physician has said otherwise.
- Bring your latest blood sugar / HbA1c report if you have one (the doctor reviews it; the front desk cannot interpret it).
- Bring a driver — your pupils will be dilated (see *Dilated eye exam: what to expect*).

## How long does it take?
About 2–3 hours including dilation and imaging.
