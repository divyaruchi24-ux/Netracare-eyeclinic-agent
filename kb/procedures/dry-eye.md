# Dry eye clinic — FAQ

## What happens at a dry eye clinic visit?
The doctor assesses the tear film and eyelids using painless tests (tear break-up time, tear volume, and
eyelid gland imaging). The visit takes about 45 minutes.

## Who do I see?
**Dr. Ananya Rao** (Cornea & Refractive Surgery).

## Should I use eye drops before the visit?
Please don't start, stop or change any drops on your own. Bring the drops you currently use; the doctor will
advise what to do. The front desk and assistant cannot recommend drops.

## Contact lens wearers
Please wear your glasses to the visit and bring your lenses and lens case.
