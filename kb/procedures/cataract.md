# Cataract evaluation and surgery — FAQ

## What is a cataract?
A cataract is a clouding of the natural lens inside the eye. Only an eye examination can confirm whether
someone has a cataract and whether surgery is advisable.

## Who do I see?
Cataract consultations are done by **Dr. Kavya Nair**.

## What happens at the consultation?
- A 30-minute visit including a dilated exam and lens-implant measurements (biometry).
- You will receive written surgery-day instructions if surgery is planned.

## What lens options are available?
NetraCare offers monofocal, toric and multifocal lens implants. Your surgeon will discuss which options fit
your eyes and needs; prices differ by lens type (see the *NetraCare price list*).

## How long is the surgery?
Typically 15–30 minutes per eye; plan for about 3–4 hours at the clinic including preparation and recovery.

## Will someone need to accompany me?
Yes. Please bring an adult companion who can take you home.
