# Squint (strabismus) and lazy eye evaluation — FAQ

## What is a squint evaluation?
An assessment of how the eyes are aligned and work together, plus a check of vision in each eye.

## Who do I see?
**Dr. Arjun Menon** — for children and for adults with squint.

## Booking for a child
- A parent or legal guardian must book and attend.
- Bring any previous glasses and reports.
- Dilating drops are commonly used; allow 1.5–2 hours.

## Sudden double vision
New, sudden double vision in an adult or child should not wait for a routine appointment — contact the clinic
emergency line or go to the nearest emergency department.
