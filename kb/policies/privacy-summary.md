# Privacy summary

- NetraCare uses your contact and appointment details only to provide care and send appointment messages.
- Only you (and guardians you have authorised, or your legal guardian if you are under 18) can see your
  appointments through the patient portal or assistant.
- The assistant will never share another person's information.
- Conversations with the assistant are logged for quality and safety, with phone numbers and email addresses masked.
- You can ask the front desk for a copy of your data or to correct it.
