# Appointment cancellation and rescheduling policy

- You can cancel or reschedule **free of charge up to 24 hours** before your appointment, online or through
  the assistant.
- Changes **within 24 hours** of the appointment are possible but need front-desk approval. You will be told
  once staff have reviewed the request.
- After **three missed appointments without notice**, future bookings are reviewed by the front desk before
  confirmation.
- Surgery dates have separate rescheduling rules explained at your consultation.
- Parents/guardians can cancel or reschedule appointments for children they manage.
