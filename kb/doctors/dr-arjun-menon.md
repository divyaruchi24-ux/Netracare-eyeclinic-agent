# Dr. Arjun Menon

**Specialty:** Pediatric Ophthalmology & Strabismus
**Languages:** English, Malayalam, Kannada, Hindi
**OPD days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday (09:00–17:00); Saturday (09:00–13:00).

## About
Dr. Menon sees children from infancy through age 17, and adults with squint (strabismus).

## Patients typically see Dr. Menon for
- First eye check-up for babies and toddlers
- School vision screening follow-ups and spectacle checks for children
- Squint (eyes not aligned) evaluation in children and adults
- Lazy eye (amblyopia) follow-up and patching reviews

## Booking notes
- A parent or legal guardian must book for patients under 18 and accompany them to the visit.
- Children's visits often include eye drops that dilate the pupils; the visit can take 1.5–2 hours.
- Saturday mornings are popular with school-age children — book early.
