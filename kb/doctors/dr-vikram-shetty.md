# Dr. Vikram Shetty

**Specialty:** Retina & Vitreous
**Languages:** English, Kannada, Tulu, Hindi
**OPD days:** Tuesday and Thursday (09:00–18:00); Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday mornings (09:00–13:00).

## About
Dr. Shetty heads the retina service. His clinic focuses on conditions affecting the back of the eye.

## Patients typically see Dr. Shetty for
- Diabetic eye screening and follow-up
- New floaters or flashes of light (after urgent triage by the front desk)
- Follow-up after retinal detachment treatment
- Macular conditions, including age-related macular degeneration follow-up
- Retinal imaging (OCT) reviews

## Booking notes
- Most retina visits include a **dilated eye exam**. Plan for 2–3 hours at the clinic and do not drive yourself home — see *Dilated eye exam: what to expect*.
- Sudden new floaters with flashes, or a curtain/shadow over vision, should **not** wait for a routine appointment. Call the clinic emergency line immediately.
