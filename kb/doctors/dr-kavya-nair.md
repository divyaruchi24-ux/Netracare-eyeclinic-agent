# Dr. Kavya Nair

**Specialty:** Cataract, General Ophthalmology & Oculoplasty
**Languages:** English, Malayalam, Hindi, Kannada
**OPD days:** Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday, Saturday (09:00–18:00). Thursday is reserved for surgeries.

## About
Dr. Nair leads the cataract and general ophthalmology service. If you are not sure which specialist to
see, a general eye check-up with Dr. Nair is a good starting point — she will refer you within the clinic
if needed.

## Patients typically see Dr. Nair for
- Cataract evaluation and surgery planning
- Routine comprehensive eye check-ups and spectacle prescriptions
- Droopy eyelids (ptosis), eyelid bumps, and tear-duct concerns
- Concerns that do not clearly fit another specialty

## Booking notes
- A cataract surgery consultation is a 30-minute visit and includes measurements for the lens implant.
- Surgery-day fasting and medication instructions are given at the consultation — see *Before your surgery*.
