# Dr. Ananya Rao

**Specialty:** Cornea & Refractive Surgery
**Languages:** English, Kannada, Telugu
**OPD days:** Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday (09:00–18:00); Saturday (09:00–13:00). Wednesday is reserved for surgeries.

## About
Dr. Rao leads the cornea and refractive service at NetraCare. She sees patients who are interested in
spectacle-free vision procedures such as LASIK and SMILE, and patients with corneal conditions.

## Patients typically see Dr. Rao for
- Evaluation for LASIK, SMILE or other refractive procedures
- Keratoconus follow-up
- Dry eye clinic visits
- Contact-lens related concerns
- Corneal infection or injury follow-up (after initial emergency care)

## Booking notes
- A refractive-surgery evaluation is a 30-minute **new patient** visit.
- Soft contact-lens wearers are asked to stop wearing lenses before the evaluation — see *Contact lens pause before evaluation*.
