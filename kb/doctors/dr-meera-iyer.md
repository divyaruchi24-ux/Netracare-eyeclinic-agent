# Dr. Meera Iyer

**Specialty:** Glaucoma
**Languages:** English, Tamil, Kannada
**OPD days:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday (09:00–18:00); Saturday (09:00–13:00).

## About
Dr. Iyer runs the glaucoma clinic, which focuses on long-term monitoring of eye pressure and the optic nerve.

## Patients typically see Dr. Iyer for
- Glaucoma screening, especially with a family history of glaucoma
- Regular eye-pressure monitoring visits
- Visual field and optic nerve (OCT) test reviews
- Second opinions on an existing glaucoma diagnosis

## Booking notes
- Please bring all current eye-drop bottles and previous test reports to every visit. Do not stop or change any medication without speaking to your doctor.
- Visual field testing adds about 30 minutes to the visit.
