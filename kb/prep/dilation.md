# Preparing for a visit with dilating drops

- Arrange a driver or taxi — **do not drive** for 4–6 hours after dilation.
- Bring sunglasses.
- Allow 2–3 hours for the visit.
- Bring your current glasses, all medicines/eye drops you use, and previous eye reports.
- You can eat and drink normally before the visit.
