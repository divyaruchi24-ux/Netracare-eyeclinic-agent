# Contact lens pause before evaluation

Contact lenses change the shape of the cornea temporarily, which can affect measurements.
Before a **refractive surgery evaluation** or a **cataract consultation**, please stop wearing lenses for:

| Lens type | Stop wearing at least |
|---|---|
| Soft lenses | 1 week before |
| Toric soft lenses | 2 weeks before |
| Rigid gas-permeable (RGP) lenses | 3 weeks before |

Wear your glasses during this time. If you are unsure which type you use, bring the lens box to the clinic
or call the front desk.
