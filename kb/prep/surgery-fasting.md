# Before your surgery — general preparation

Your surgeon's team gives you **personal written instructions** at your consultation; those always take priority
over this general page.

- **Fasting:** follow the exact fasting time on your instruction sheet. If you were not given one, call the clinic.
- **Medicines:** do not stop or change any medicine (including blood thinners or diabetes medicines) unless your
  doctor has told you to. If unsure, call the clinic.
- Arrive 60 minutes before your scheduled time.
- Bring an adult companion to take you home.
- Wear comfortable clothes; no eye make-up, face cream or jewellery.
- Bring your ID, insurance documents and the consent form if already signed.
